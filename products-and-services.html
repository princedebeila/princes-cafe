Our Products & Services

Discover what makes Princes Cafe special. From expertly crafted coffee to freshly baked goods and catering services, we've got everything to elevate your cafe experience.

Premium Coffee

Signature Espresso Blends

Our house-blend espresso is carefully roasted and sourced from quality coffee beans. Whether you prefer a classic Americano, rich Cappuccino, or smooth Latte, each cup is expertly prepared by our trained baristas to bring out the best flavors.

Specialty Coffee Drinks

Looking for something unique? Try our specialty drinks like Vanilla Macchiato, Caramel Macchiato, or seasonal favorites. Each drink is customizable to your taste preferences—dairy-free, extra shot, or syrup of your choice.

Cold Brew & Iced Coffee

Perfect for warm days, our cold brew is smooth, refreshing, and made fresh daily. We also offer iced lattes, iced cappuccinos, and cold brew with a variety of flavor additions to keep you cool and energized.

Freshly Baked Pastries

Croissants & Pastries

Our pastries are baked fresh every morning. Enjoy butter croissants, chocolate croissants, almond croissants, and seasonal varieties. Each pastry is flaky, buttery, and the perfect companion to your coffee.

Muffins & Scones

Indulge in our homemade muffins in flavors like blueberry, chocolate chip, and lemon poppy. Our scones are perfect for a light snack, served warm with jam and cream on request.

Cakes & Brownies

From rich chocolate brownies to fluffy sponge cakes, our sweet treats are baked with quality ingredients. Perfect for a dessert with your coffee or to take home for later.

Food Menu

Breakfast Options

Start your day right with our breakfast selection. Choose from toasted sandwiches, eggs on toast, avocado on sourdough, or granola bowls with yogurt and fresh fruit. All served with your choice of beverage.

Light Bites & Salads

Perfect for lunch or a mid-day snack. Our menu includes fresh salads, wraps, quiches, and sandwiches made with quality ingredients. Options for vegetarian and health-conscious customers available.

Soups & Seasonal Specials

Our daily soup selection changes seasonally, featuring hearty vegetable soups, creamy tomato, and warm broths. Ask our staff about today's special—it pairs perfectly with a fresh pastry.

Event Hosting & Catering

Private Events

Looking for a venue for your small gathering, birthday, or celebration? Princes Cafe offers a warm, welcoming space perfect for intimate events. We can customize the atmosphere to suit your occasion.

Catering Services

Let us handle your refreshments. We offer catering packages including coffee, pastries, sandwiches, and desserts. Perfect for office meetings, workshops, or celebrations. Minimum order applies.

Custom Orders

Have a special request? Contact us for custom orders including custom cakes, bulk pastry orders, or personalized catering packages. We're happy to work with you to make your event memorable.

Why Choose Our Products & Services

  • Quality Ingredients: We use premium, ethically sourced ingredients in everything we offer.
  • Fresh Daily: Pastries and food are prepared fresh every morning.
  • Expert Baristas: Our trained staff ensures every coffee is made to perfection.
  • Customization: Most items can be customized to your dietary preferences and taste.
  • Affordable Pricing: Premium quality at prices that won't break the bank.
  • Welcoming Atmosphere: A space where you can relax, work, or connect with friends.
